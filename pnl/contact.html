Postdoctoral Position in Audiovisual Speaker Tracking

At least one postdoctoral position will be available starting January 2027 under the supervision of Prof. DeLiang Wang in the School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. The postdoc project seeks multimodal, multi-channel algorithms to perform speaker tracking in noisy and reverberant scenarios, in order to elevate separation, diarization, localization, and recognition performances in real-world environments. More specifically, the position holder will investigate deep learning methods for robust speaker tracking by leveraging audio and visual inputs, as well as foundation models. The project also aims for causal neural networks to facilitate real-time implementation.

The appointment will be for two years. The position carries competitive compensation, and the successful candidate will join a world-leading laboratory in developing the deep learning based approach to speech separation (the cocktail party problem). To apply, send a cover letter, CV, and contact information for 2-3 references, to DeLiang Wang.
