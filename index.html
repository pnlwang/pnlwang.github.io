DeLiang WANG, Presidental Chair Professor

I am with the School of Data Science (SDS) at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

I direct the Perception and Neurodynamics Lab (PNL), which has programs and demos for many of our algorithms on machine perception. In addition, Guy Brown and I edited a book: "Computational Auditory Scene Analysis: Principles, Algorithms, and Applications" published by Wiley/IEEE Press in 2006, available in IEEE Xplore.

In March 2017, IEEE Spectrum ran a cover story on our work towards solving the cocktail party (speech separation) problem.
